# Competition Overview

## Theme: Combine to NFL Performance (2027)

Every spring, college football's top prospects gather in Indianapolis for the NFL Scouting Combine. Traditional evaluation relies on stopwatch timings (40-yard dash, short shuttle, 3-cone drill, vertical jump). Player tracking sensors now capture the full movement details during drills.

**Goal:** Build an analytical framework that uncovers **non-obvious, actionable linkages** between 10 Hz Combine sensor tracking data and future regular-season NFL game performance.

### What "Good" Looks Like

- Look **beyond conventional scouting wisdom**
- Identify **novel signals** that help coaches/evaluators:
  - Find hidden talent
  - Avoid costly draft busts
  - Design position-specific player development protocols
- Make insights **actionable and accessible** to coaches, scouts, and front offices
